## What Home Alone: Anomalies Is, According to Roblox

The official Roblox page (place 87468080405188) describes a specific premise. You are home alone, and visitors keep arriving at the door. Not everyone is who they claim to be. You have to decide who to trust and survive the night.

The creators are listed as 3 AM Productions and Freeground. Roblox's record shows the place was created on May 4, 2026, last updated on October 10, 2026, supports up to 18 players per server, and has around 208 million visits. Those figures come from the platform's own listing and will drift over time, so treat them as a snapshot rather than a fixed number.

The advertised loop is deliberately small: check cameras, listen to what visitors say, open the door if someone needs help, turn off the porch light if something is off, and survive until morning to earn cash. There are also troll items meant to be used on friends, which signals the game is built for shared lobbies rather than solo tension alone.

## How to Approach a Night Sensibly

Start by treating the camera as your first source and visitor dialogue as your second. The official description puts both before any decision, which suggests the game wants you to gather information rather than guess at the door. The porch light is described as the response when something is off, so it functions as a rejection tool rather than a decoration.

Play a few rounds with the goal of observing rather than surviving. Note which visitors make claims that turn out to be false, and note which ones were simply unusual without being dangerous. Because the game supports up to 18 players, expect noise: other players, their troll items, and their choices can change how a night unfolds. Nothing here guarantees survival, and the official page makes no such promise either.
