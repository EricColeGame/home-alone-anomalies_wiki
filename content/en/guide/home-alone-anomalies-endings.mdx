## Reading Visitors Instead of Guessing

The design pushes you toward evidence rather than instinct. Cameras give you a view before anyone reaches the door. What a visitor says gives you a second signal. The porch light gives you a third option: leaving it off is a way to decline without necessarily opening up.

The practical reasoning is straightforward. A visitor who needs help is someone you are meant to let in. A visitor who is "off" in some way is someone you are meant to turn away, and the porch light is the tool the game gives you for that decision. Because the official page does not enumerate anomaly types or tell you exactly what makes a visitor suspicious, the reliable method is to compare what you see on camera with what you hear at the door. When those two things disagree, that disagreement is your warning.

Third-party guides describe various visitor behaviors and anomaly categories, but those are community observations, not developer documentation. They can be useful starting points, yet they can also go stale after updates. The safest habit is to verify any specific visitor behavior yourself in a live session rather than trusting a list someone posted months ago.

## Why the Game Works as a Trust Exercise

Most horror-adjacent Roblox games rely on jump scares or chase sequences. Home Alone Anomalies leans on a quieter pressure: you have limited information, a door you control, and a night that has to end. That structure is what makes the cash reward feel earned. You are not surviving because you ran fast. You are surviving because you read the situation correctly.

The 18-player server cap is also worth noting, because it changes the social texture. Large servers mean more chances for friends to join and use troll items, which the official page explicitly advertises. If you are playing seriously, a smaller group or a solo session removes some of that noise. If you are playing for fun, the troll items are part of the intended experience, not a bug.

One caution: because the official page does not describe a complete list of visitors or anomaly types, do not assume a guide's roster is exhaustive. Updates can add costumed visitors, as UPDATE 12 did, which means older lists may be incomplete by design.
