## How the trust decision is supposed to work

The official page frames the game around a binary judgment repeated across a night: help or refuse. The camera and the porch light are your two tools for gathering information before that judgment. The visitor's dialogue is the third input.

What the page does not do is tell you which cues reliably indicate a threat. That gap is where most community confusion lives. Third-party guides describe various approaches to reading visitor behavior, but those are player observations rather than developer documentation. They can be useful, and some of them are probably accurate, but they can also be outdated after a patch like Update 12 that explicitly introduces costumed visitors.

A practical way to think about it: the game gives you three observation channels and one action. Use all three channels before acting. If the camera view, the dialogue, and the porch light state are all consistent with a normal visitor, opening the door is the intended play. If any one of them conflicts with the others, that conflict is the signal. This is reasoning from the advertised mechanics, not a guaranteed strategy, and it will not save you every night.
