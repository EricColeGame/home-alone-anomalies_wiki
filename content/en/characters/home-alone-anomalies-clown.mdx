Home Alone Anomalies is a Roblox social-deduction horror experience built around a simple but stressful premise: you are alone in a house, visitors keep arriving, and not all of them are telling the truth. The official Roblox page (verified 2026-10-14) describes the loop clearly: check cameras, listen to what visitors say, open the door if they need help, and turn off the porch light if something is off. Survive the night and you earn cash. Created by the groups 3 AM Productions and Freeground, the place was published on 2026-05-04 and last updated 2026-10-10, and it supports up to 18 players per server.

That 18-player cap matters more than it looks. This is not a solo scripted horror game where a jump scare is waiting on a timer. With a full server, several people are juggling the same door, the same porch light, and the same decisions, which is exactly where the tension (and the arguing) comes from.

## What the Game Actually Asks You to Do

The advertised loop is short enough to memorize: watch, listen, judge, act.

| Step | Advertised action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check the cameras | Gives you information before you commit to anything |
| 2 | Listen to what the visitor says | Dialogue is your main clue about intent |
| 3 | Open the door if they need help | Helping appears to be the intended "correct" response |
| 4 | Turn off the porch light if something is off | The light is your primary defensive tool |
| 5 | Survive the night | Completion is the win condition, rewarded with cash |

Notice what is *not* on that list: no combat, no weapons, no chase mechanics are advertised. The official description frames the entire game around observation and a binary choice. That means your success depends on reading behavior, not reflexes.

A practical reading of the loop: treat the camera as your baseline, not your answer. If a visitor's story matches what you can see and hear, opening the door is the advertised helpful move. If something contradicts, the porch light is the advertised way to shut the situation down without opening anything. The design deliberately gives you an "out" that does not require you to gamble.

## Community Observations vs. Documented Facts

This is where most guides go wrong, so it is worth being blunt about the tiers of information.

**Documented by the developer (official page):** the door/camera/porch-light loop, cash rewards for surviving, 18-player servers, trolling items for friends, and the UPDATE 12 Halloween contents listed above.

**Observed by third-party guides:** specific visitor behavior patterns, which dialogue lines tend to signal a threat, and practical tips for new players. These are secondary observations from people who played the game, not developer documentation. They can be genuinely useful, but they are subject to change with any update and should never be treated as gospel — especially after a patch like UPDATE 12 that explicitly adds new costumed visitors.

**Not documented anywhere official:** a complete list of anomalies, named visitor characters with stats, house layouts, multiple endings, hotkeys, or promo codes. If a page claims to give you "all anomaly names" or "working codes," treat it as unverified at best. The official page documents no codes, so assume none exist until the developers say otherwise.

If you want to verify something, do it the boring reliable way: play a round and watch, or read the official description at https://www.roblox.com/games/87468080405188/Home-Alone-Anomalies, which is the only source that reflects what the developers actually shipped.

## Practical Reasoning for New Players

Because the game is about judgment rather than execution, the useful advice is about *process*. A few habits that follow directly from the advertised mechanics:

**Slow down before you open the door.** The porch light exists precisely so you have a non-committal option. Using it costs you nothing except the visitor's help opportunity, so there is no reason to rush a decision you cannot undo.

**Cross-check sources of information.** The camera and the visitor's dialogue are two separate inputs. The design invites you to compare them. A mismatch between what you see and what you hear is the most obvious signal the game gives you.

**Communicate in a full server.** With up to 18 players, you are not the only one at the door. Coordinating with other players — who is checking cameras, who already talked to this visitor — is a legitimate strategy the format supports, even if the official page does not spell it out as a feature.

**Expect the Halloween visitors to be different.** UPDATE 12 added costumed visitors. If your mental model of "normal" behavior was built before the update, it needs recalibrating. Old pattern-matching habits may mislead you during the event.

**Do not assume survival is guaranteed.** Nothing in the documentation promises a win. You earn cash by surviving the night, which means failure is possible and probably frequent while you are learning.

No. They are separate Roblox experiences. Home Alone Anomalies is the door-and-porch-light survival game by 3 AM Productions and Freeground. Do not mix up guides written for one with the other.

Nothing on the official Roblox page documents any codes. If you see a code list, verify it in-game before assuming it works.

Up to 18, per the official listing.

Cash, according to the official description. No specific amounts are documented, so treat any quoted figure as unverified.
